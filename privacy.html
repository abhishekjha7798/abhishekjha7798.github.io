Privacy policy

algo-trade-app is used only by its owner. It asks for the Google Drive scope drive.file, so it can reach only the files and folders it creates itself, and nothing else in the Drive.

It uploads log files from the owner's machines into the owner's own Drive. It does not read other Drive data, does not share data with anyone, and stores no data anywhere else.

Access can be removed at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
